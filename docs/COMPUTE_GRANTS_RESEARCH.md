> **Purpose:** Map programs (like NVIDIA Inception) that give early-stage startups free GPU/cloud compute, credits, and support — tailored to **Bindlix**: pre-seed, 2 founders, Kingston ON Canada, GPU-heavy de novo protein design (AlphaFold2, RFdiffusion, ProteinMPNN, BindCraft, Boltz-2, PyRosetta, GROMACS on A100/H100), already pursuing NVIDIA Inception.

## TL;DR — what to do, in order

1. **Finish NVIDIA Inception → immediately apply to Nebius AI Lift.** ✅ Inception members get up to **$150K cloud + $10K inference credits** + early Blackwell access. This is the single most GPU-relevant, highest-value program for your A100/H100 workloads, and it stacks on your Inception membership.
2. **Self-serve cloud credits in parallel** (no referral needed): AWS Activate Founders (~$5K), Azure (~$5K), Google Cloud. These stack with each other.
3. **At pre-seed raise, get an investor/accelerator referral** → unlocks the **$100K–$200K** AWS/Azure tiers. Favor VCs that are AWS/Microsoft/Google partners.
4. **Non-dilutive Canadian funding now:** NRC IRAP (cash for R&D), Vector FastLane (talent). Queue the **Canada AI Compute Access Fund** for after you have revenue / Series A.

## Tier 1 — Best fit for Bindlix (GPU-native, high value)

### Nebius AI Lift ✅ Verified
- **Provides:** Up to **$150K USD cloud credits + $10K USD inference credits**; early access to NVIDIA Blackwell GPUs; technical support.
- **Eligibility:** Must be an **NVIDIA Inception member** (this is the unlock — you're already on that path). Perks are **gated to your first 3 months** of Inception membership, so timing matters.
- **AI/biotech fit:** GPU cloud built for AI training/inference — directly suits AlphaFold2/Boltz-2/RFdiffusion. Nebius actively markets to biotech (has published next-gen biotech research case studies).
- **Geography:** Cloud service, no Canada exclusion found.
- **Stacking:** Yes — independent of your home-cluster (Alliance Canada) usage and cloud-provider credits.
- **Action:** Apply the moment Inception is approved; don't let the 3-month window lapse.
- **Source:** nebius.com/blog/posts/ai-lift-startups-innovation-with-nvidia (primary, NVIDIA GTC 2025)

### CoreWeave Startup Accelerator ⚠️ Terms from 2022 — re-confirm
- **Provides:** Cloud credits + ongoing usage discounts; access to **10+ NVIDIA GPU SKUs including A100 and H100**.
- **Eligibility:** Early-stage startups (specific 2025 thresholds not re-verified this pass).
- **AI/biotech fit:** Pure GPU cloud — strong technical fit for your stack.
- **Geography:** US-headquartered provider; confirm Canadian eligibility.
- **Stacking:** Generally yes (separate vendor).
- **Caveat:** Program details are from a 2022 announcement; **verify it still runs and current credit amount** before counting on it.
- **Source:** coreweave.com/blog/coreweave-launches-startup-accelerator-program (primary, 2022)

## Tier 2 — Major cloud-provider credits (broad, stackable)

### AWS Activate ✅ Verified
- **Provides:**
  - **Founders tier:** up to **$5K** (self-serve, no investor needed)
  - **Portfolio tier:** up to **$200K** (for startups in a partnered VC/accelerator/incubator portfolio)
  - **Invite-only "Credits for AI" tier:** **$200K+** for AI-focused startups
- **Eligibility:** **Pre-Series B**, company **<10 years old**, must be on an **AWS paid-tier account** (not just free tier), have a functioning website/product. Larger tiers require affiliation with a **partnered investor/accelerator**.
- **AI/biotech fit:** General-purpose; AWS has HealthOmics / life-sciences services and GPU instances (P4/P5 = A100/H100).
- **Geography:** Canada eligible.
- **Stacking:** Yes — commonly run alongside Azure + GCP credits.
- **Bindlix status:** Qualifies on stage/age for the Founders tier today; Portfolio/AI tiers need a VC referral.
- **Source:** aws.amazon.com/startups/credits/ (primary)

### Microsoft for Startups (Founders Hub) ✅ Verified
- **Provides:**
  - **Investor-referred:** Azure credits starting at **$100K** + priority access to investor-portfolio GPU clusters
  - **Non-affiliated (self-serve):** up to **$5K** Azure
  - Plus: free dev tools, GitHub, OpenAI/Azure AI access, technical support
- **Eligibility (changed July 2, 2025):** The **$100K+ tier now requires a referral from an affiliated investor/accelerator.** The smaller self-serve tier is open to qualifying startups. *(Note: the deep-research pass killed a specific blog description of the $5K-tier eligibility rules — so confirm exact self-serve criteria on Microsoft's page rather than third-party summaries.)*
- **AI/biotech fit:** General; Azure has strong AI/HPC GPU offerings (ND-series = A100/H100) and life-sciences tooling.
- **Geography:** Canada eligible.
- **Stacking:** Yes.
- **Source:** learn.microsoft.com/en-us/startups/changes-microsoft-for-startups (primary, July 2 2025)

### Google for Startups Cloud Program ⚠️ Confirm current AI-track amount
- **Provides:** Historically up to **$200K** GCP credits ($100K/yr × 2 years) for funded startups; **AI-focused startups have qualified for higher amounts** (some tiers cited up to ~$350K); standard/seed tier is smaller (~$2K). Includes GPU **and TPU** access.
- **Eligibility:** Incorporated, typically Seed–Series A, AI-focus boosts the tier. Standard tier open to early startups.
- **AI/biotech fit:** GCP has Vertex AI, GPU/TPU, and life-sciences/genomics offerings.
- **Geography:** Canada eligible.
- **Stacking:** Yes.
- **Caveat:** Exact 2025–2026 AI-track figure **not independently verified this pass** — check cloud.google.com/startup/ai.
- **Source:** cloud.google.com/startup/ai (primary, but claim not verified) + creditforstartups.com (blog)

### Oracle for Startups ⚠️ Unverified
- **Provides:** Historically OCI cloud credits + steep usage discounts (past iterations cited up to ~$300K cloud credits); GPU instances available.
- **Eligibility / Geography / current status:** **Not verified this pass** — Oracle has restructured this program before. Confirm directly.
- **Note:** Lower priority for Bindlix given the others above.

## Tier 3 — AI-model credits (limited relevance to protein design)

### Anthropic Startup Program ✅ Verified
- **Provides:** Claude **API credits**, elevated rate limits, technical resources; **up to ~$250K** for VC-nominated startups (no fixed self-serve amount stated).
- **Eligibility:** Early-stage; VC nomination unlocks the largest credits. **Canada eligible** (not excluded in official terms).
- **Fit for Bindlix:** **LLM/API only** — does *not* help GPU protein-design compute. Useful only if you build LLM features (e.g., research copilots, agentic tooling).
- **Source:** anthropic.com/startup-program-official-terms (primary)

### OpenAI / Hugging Face ⚠️ Unverified, low fit
- **OpenAI:** Startup credits mostly flow through partnered accelerators (e.g., Converge); API/LLM-only.
- **Hugging Face:** Compute grants, ZeroGPU, enterprise credits for select programs — inference/model-hosting oriented, not large-scale structure-prediction training.
- **Fit for Bindlix:** Minimal for your core workloads.

## Tier 4 — Canada-specific (your geography)

### Canada AI Compute Access Fund (ISED) ✅ Verified — strong fit *later*
- **Provides:** $300M CAD national fund; reimburses **up to 66% of Canadian-provider / up to 50% of non-Canadian-provider** cloud AI-compute costs. Projects **$100K–$5M over 3 years**.
- **Eligibility:** R&D team based in Canada doing AI compute work; **requires the applicant to be revenue-generating or at Series A.** ⚠️ **This excludes pre-seed Bindlix today.**
- **AI/biotech fit:** **Life sciences is a named priority sector** — excellent alignment once you qualify.
- **Timing:** **Last call closed July 31, 2025.** Watch for the next intake.
- **Action:** Park until you have revenue / a Series A; then this is a top target given the life-sciences priority.
- **Source:** ised-isde.canada.ca/.../ai-compute-access-fund (primary)

### NRC IRAP (Industrial Research Assistance Program) ✅ Verified
- **Provides:** Non-dilutive **R&D funding** + advisory services from Industrial Technology Advisors. **Not direct compute credits**, but cash that can fund compute, salaries, and contractors.
- **Eligibility:** **Incorporated, for-profit, Canadian company with <500 full-time employees**, pursuing technology-driven innovation. **Bindlix qualifies.**
- **AI/biotech fit:** Sector-agnostic; routinely funds AI and biotech R&D.
- **Action:** Engage an ITA early — IRAP relationships take time and gate larger project funding.
- **Source:** nrc.canada.ca/.../financial-support-technology-innovation (primary)

### Vector Institute — FastLane (AI Startup & Scale-Up Program) ✅ Verified
- **Provides:** **1–2 embedded ML Associates** for a **~4-month proof-of-concept**; technical/talent support and Vector ecosystem access. **No cloud or GPU credits.**
- **Eligibility:** **Canada-only** (headquartered in Canada); **pre-revenue is OK** — fits early-stage. Selective intake.
- **AI/biotech fit:** ML talent for your pipeline; Vector has deep ML/AI bench.
- **Action:** Apply for hands-on ML help, not compute.
- **Source:** vectorinstitute.ai/programs/ai-startup-and-scale-up-program/ (primary)

### Digital Research Alliance of Canada (Compute Canada) — what you already use
- **Provides:** The academic HPC you run on now (Frontenac/Nibi/Narval, A100/H100). Allocations are **research/academic**, tied to a PI/institution.
- **Note:** A startup-specific commercial track was **not verified** this pass. As you commercialize, academic allocations may no longer cover the work — which is exactly why the cloud-credit programs above matter.

## Tier 5 — Accelerators that bundle cloud credits (equity / competitive)

### Y Combinator ⚠️ Equity, competitive
- **Provides:** ~$500K investment + a large perk bundle including **~$200K AWS** credits plus Azure/GCP credits and many SaaS deals.
- **Trade-off:** Takes **equity**; highly competitive admission. The cloud perks come bundled, not standalone.

### Techstars (and healthcare/biotech accelerators) ⚠️ Equity, competitive
- **Provides:** Investment + mentorship + cloud-credit perks similar to YC.
- **Note:** Several **healthcare/life-sciences-specific accelerators** exist (worth scanning if you want domain mentorship alongside compute). Equity-based.
